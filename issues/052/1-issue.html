01  Nvidia investor release + CNBC, Nov 23 · 2,316 HN points this morning

🟢 Jensen Huang is Nvidia’s CEO again - 12 days after resigning, by unanimous board vote, with Washington’s input.

The board that said reinstatement was not on the table reversed over one weekend: it “received input from senior levels of the United States government,” concluded the return “poses no national security risk,” and watched the stock close up 31%. The investigation continues; three days of congressional testimony begin December 1.
SCENE

On November 13 Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia’s CEO after reports of multiple extramarital affairs, including one with a woman the New York Times says was in contact with a Chinese Ministry of State Security officer during at least part of the relationship. The board said it was not considering bringing him back; interim CEO Colette Kress repeated that position to CNBC. Then, one weekend: senior government officials briefed the board late last week that no national security laws were violated, per CNBC’s sources; Huang and the board held their first conversation Saturday and decided Sunday; Monday morning the release went out. Trump told CNBC he had been speaking to the board “directly and indirectly,” said he talked to Huang on Sunday and asked him to promise to “work hard,” then celebrated the “correct and proper decision” on his social network. Musk, Gerstner and Baker had lobbied for the return; Musk called the board’s “mistake” corrected.

THE THING

The official record, from the investor release: the vote was unanimous; the board is “satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candor and ability to lead NVIDIA”; Kress returns to CFO only. The independent committee’s investigation - outside counsel Wachtell, Lipton retained - “will proceed to conclusion,” with Huang “committed to cooperating with it.” New apparatus: a National Security and Export Control Committee of independent directors, and Nvidia’s first Chief Compliance Officer, a search now open. The calendar that matters: December 1, a closed-door transcribed interview with the House Foreign Affairs panel and two other committees; December 2, general counsel Tim Teter; December 3, Huang as the sole public witness at a Senate Banking hearing titled “Protecting America’s AI Advantage: Oversight of U.S. Export Controls.” Senator Warren’s response: “the reckless decision to bring Jensen Huang back as CEO does not change the fact that Mr. Huang, NVIDIA executives, and the company’s board still owe Congress answers.”

✂️ STEAL IT

Watch the governance mechanics, not the drama. A board down a third of its market value reversed a stated position in 72 hours once two inputs arrived: a government all-clear and a direct line to the founder. If your infrastructure bets on Nvidia - it does - the durable changes are the compliance apparatus and the December 1-3 testimony, not the CEO’s surname. Export-control risk on your GPU roadmap now runs through a Senate hearing; treat supply assumptions as live until December 3.

Watch  VERDICT

Three testimony days in nine days will move this story more than the reinstatement did. This fails you if you read the +31% close as the ending: the board’s own investigation is still running, the “alleged spy” reporting remains outlet-sourced, and Congress has not asked its first question.

02  z.ai research blog, Nov 23 · 1,087 HN points this morning · vendor benchmarks

🔁 GLM-5.4: z.ai’s first loop transformer flagship - a 750B model that runs 28 of its layers twice, claiming 2T-3T-class coding.

Instead of adding parameters, GLM-5.4 loops a weight-shared block of the GLM-5 base for a second pass: per-token gates keep activations stable, and a shared-plus-sliding-window KV cache keeps the extra pass cheap at 1M context. Every number is the vendor’s; outside evals have not landed.
SCENE

Three months of scaling loop depth and model size in parallel, says z.ai, produced its first flagship on a Loop Transformer - and its pitch for “Generalized Coding”: code as the foundation for longer-horizon work, from large-scale software engineering to cybersecurity, 3D modeling and physical control. The claim on the tin: intelligence comparable to 2T-3T parameter models. It is live for GLM Coding Plan users now.

THE THING

The architecture in one breath: a standard transformer runs each layer once; GLM-5.4 loops 28 layers of the GLM-5 base for two iterations. Three changes make that survivable at 750B. Gated progressive updates: per-token gates write candidate updates into the residual stream proportionally - the first pass writes conservatively and builds global context, the second opens the gates - because overwriting hidden states directly blew up activation norms. Hybrid KV cache: the second loop reuses the sparse KV selected in the first pass and adds only its own sliding-window KV, so the extra loop costs one window instead of a full cache - the reason 1M context stays affordable. Sparse attention index reuse: the second pass reuses the first pass’s attention indices at zero indexing cost, performing “nearly identically” to recomputing. Validation was staged 1B, 4B, 30B, 750B; on a 30B iso-parameter ablation with ~10% more training compute, the looped version showed clear gains. Their own listed limitation: it gets caught in overly long “marathon thinking” loops - slower, though they say the prolonged reflection does improve accuracy.

✂️ STEAL IT

Read the KV-cache trick even if you never touch GLM. Recurrent depth just became flagship-credible, and the path matters: they adapted an already pretrained base into a looped model through continued training, because scratch-trained looped models still lag at equal pretraining compute. Parameter count is officially not the only lever - effective depth per parameter is now a shipping product, not a paper.

Try today  VERDICT

If you have a GLM Coding Plan, point one real refactor at it and watch for the marathon-thinking failure mode they flag themselves. Otherwise watch: every number in the launch is z.ai’s own, chosen by z.ai, and no independent evaluation exists yet. It fails you today if you need verified parity with the 2T-3T models it claims to match.
