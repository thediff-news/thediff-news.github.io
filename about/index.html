About

One issue a day for people who build with AI.

Every issue has two sections. Viral is what blew up on X, Reddit, YouTube, Hacker News and elsewhere, with where it happened and a number. Under the radar is the long tail: little attention, real value.

Each item starts with a person or a scene, then the tool, then a way to try it, then a plain verdict: Try today, Watch, or Skip, and what makes it fail.

How we check

  • Numbers are read at the source on the day. Likes, views, stars and points are dated.
  • Claims by an author are labeled as the author's. What we cannot verify is dropped.
  • Verdicts are our judgment, and we say when we have not run the thing.
  • Sources: X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Bluesky, YouTube, Product Hunt and public LinkedIn posts.

There are no tracking pixels or analytics scripts on this site.
